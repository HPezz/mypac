# Prior scope decisions

<!-- pac:out-of-scope -->
## Out-of-Scope Decision: hosted service operation

mypac packages local agent workflows and development resources. Operating hosted storage, identity, billing, or runtime infrastructure is outside its durable product boundary. Integrations may target external services, but mypac does not become the service operator.

## Out-of-Scope Decision: unrelated mobile client

A first-party mobile client is outside the repository's workflow-package scope.
